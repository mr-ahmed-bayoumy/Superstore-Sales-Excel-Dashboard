# Superstore Sales Performance Dashboard

### Overview:

This project analyzes retail sales transaction data from the Superstore dataset using Microsoft Excel to uncover revenue trends, profitability drivers, seasonal dynamics, customer segments, and regional market distribution. The dashboard provides an interactive executive overview to support data-driven commercial strategies and margin optimization.

### Objectives:

- Track overall sales revenue, profitability, and operational losses.
- Analyze long-term growth and seasonal cycles using YoY and MoM performance metrics.
- Evaluate sales and margin performance across product categories and sub-categories.
- Compare customer purchasing patterns across Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments.
- Map geographic distribution and assess average order shipping turnaround times.

### Tools Used:

- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts
- Dynamic Slicers & Report Connections
- Advanced Formulas & Data Structuring

### Dashboard Features:

- Executive KPI Scorecards (Sales, Discounts, Profit, Loss, Volume)
- Year-over-Year (YoY) Annual Growth Tracking
- Month-over-Month (MoM) Seasonality Curve
- Sales & Profitability Breakdown by Category
- Customer Segment Distribution
- Regional Market Share Breakdown
- Operational Metrics (Orders, Customers, Products, Shipping Days)
- Interactive Multi-Field Slicers

### Key Insights:

1. Q4 Seasonality Drives Massive Revenue Spikes:
- Sales demonstrate consistent surge cycles peaking heavily in September, November, and December, confirming that back-to-school campaigns and year-end holiday shopping drive the highest transactional volume.

2. Severe Margin Compression in Furniture:
- While the "Furniture" category generates substantial gross revenue ($578K+), it produces a minimal net profit ($21K) compared to other lines, heavily impacted by high logistics overhead and aggressive pricing markdowns.

3. Technology Stands as the Core Profit Driver:
- "Technology" consistently leads performance across both revenue ($665K+) and net profitability ($111K+), making it the most resilient and lucrative category in the portfolio.

4. East and West Regions Dominate Regional Revenue:
- The West ($725K) and East ($678K) regions account for over 60% of total company revenue, while the Southern region remains an underutilized territory with significant expansion potential.

5. Consumer Tier Serves as the Backbone of Sales Volume:
- Individual Consumers generate over 50% of total orders and sales volume, significantly outpacing the Corporate and Home Office segments in both purchase count and repeat orders.

6. High Discounts Correlate Directly with Capital Losses:
- An aggregate loss of -$156K is directly tied to excessive promotional discounting (exceeding standard thresholds), demonstrating that unregulated discount margins diminish overall profitability without yielding proportional volume gains.

## Business Questions Answered

- What is the total gross revenue and net profit margin achieved across all sales cycles?
- How has annual sales performance evolved across multi-year comparisons (YoY)?
- What months experience peak sales velocity and seasonal slowdowns (MoM)?
- Which product categories generate strong top-line revenue versus bottom-line profit?
- How does revenue performance vary across different geographic territories?
- Which customer segment (Consumer, Corporate, Home Office) yields the highest transactional value?
- What is the overall impact of product discounts on net operating profit and loss?
- What is the average order turnaround time from placement to shipping?
