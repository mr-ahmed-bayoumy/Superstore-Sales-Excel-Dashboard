# Superstore Sales Performance Dashboard (Excel)

An Interactive Excel Sales Performance Dashboard analyzing revenue, profit, YoY/MoM growth, and regional performance.

An executive, interactive sales performance dashboard built in Microsoft Excel using the classic Superstore dataset. The project tracks core commercial metrics, revenue distribution, customer segments, profit, YoY/MoM growth, regional performance and growth dynamics across time.

![Dashboard Preview](Superstore_Excel_Dashboard.png)

## Key Business Insights & Metrics
- **Total Revenue & Profitability:** Summarized high-level KPIs including Total Sales, Total Profit, and Net Margin.
- **Trend & Growth Dynamics:** Tracked Year-over-Year (YoY) and Month-over-Month (MoM) performance to highlight seasonal patterns and sales peaks.
- **Product & Category Analysis:** Compared sales versus profits across product categories to pinpoint high-margin revenue drivers.
- **Geographic & Customer Breakdown:** Mapped sales share across regions and segmented performance across Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office tiers.

## Features & Implementation
- **Data Structuring & Cleaning:** Validated raw transactions, structured tables, and normalized attributes.
- **Pivot Tables & Calculated Metrics:** Built dedicated summary models powering dynamic KPI scorecards.
- **Dynamic Slicers:** Connected interactive filters across all visuals via pivot report connections.
- **Executive UI Design:** Designed a balanced, card-based canvas with customized color hierarchy for clean business consumption.
